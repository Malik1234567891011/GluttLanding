How It Works

Three steps from inspiration to dinner.

1) Import what you find

Share a recipe from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, any website, or a screenshot. If the method is only spoken in the video, Glutt listens and writes it down.

2) Check it against your kitchen

Glutt knows what is in your fridge and pantry, spots missing ingredients, suggests substitutes, and sends the rest to your grocery list.

3) Cook it with Polly

Say "Chef" and ask. Polly, your live AI chef, walks you through every step, runs your timers and can look at the pan through the camera.
