Support

Need help with Glutt? Reach us at hi@cielpm.ai.

Please include the email tied to your account and a short description of the issue so we can help quickly.

Many questions are already answered in the Glutt FAQ, and the guides cover things like using Polly with Meta glasses and importing recipes.

What to contact us about

  • Bugs or app crashes
  • Account access issues
  • Billing or subscription questions
  • Privacy and data requests
  • General product feedback

Response times

We aim to respond within 2 business days. For urgent account issues, mark your subject line as "Urgent account help".
