Mealime is shutting down. Here is what to use instead.

Mealime, the popular meal-planning app, is being discontinued on October 21, 2026. If you relied on it for weekly plans and grocery lists, here is how to keep your recipes and where to go next.

Updated September 21, 2026 By Malik Shourbaji, founder of Glutt 3 min read

Short answer

Mealime will be discontinued on October 21, 2026, and says all personal data will be deleted when the app shuts down. Its closing notice does not offer an export, so save the recipes you care about before October 21. Mealime's recipes also live on public pages at mealime.com, so you can import them into another recipe app by link. Good replacements: Meals Hub (Mealime's official successor, inside Albertsons-family grocery apps), Plan to Eat or Paprika for a classic planner, Samsung Food for free, and Glutt if you want a live AI chef to cook with.

What is happening to Mealime

Mealime's own closing page, titled "Mealime is moving to Meals Hub," says: 1

  • "Mealime will be discontinued as of October 21, 2026."
  • Pro subscribers "will not be charged any additional subscription fees" before then, and all Pro recipes and features are now available to every Mealime user.
  • Users are pointed to Meals Hub, which lives inside Albertsons Companies grocery apps such as Safeway, Albertsons, Vons, Jewel-Osco, ACME Markets and Shaw's.
  • "All personal data will be deleted when the Mealime application is shut down."

The notice says nothing about exporting recipes, meal plans or grocery lists. Plan on anything you have not copied out by October 21 being gone.

How to save your Mealime recipes before October 21

  1. List the recipes you actually cook. Most people have a dozen favorites, not hundreds. Start there.
  2. Find each one on mealime.com. Mealime's recipes are published as public web pages under mealime.com/recipes/, so a web search for the recipe name plus "Mealime" usually finds it.
  3. Import the page into your new app. Most recipe apps can import a recipe from a web link. In Glutt, share the page from Safari to Glutt, or paste the link, and it becomes a recipe with ingredients and steps.
  4. Screenshot anything you can't find. If a recipe or a note only exists inside the app, take screenshots. Glutt, and several other apps, can read a recipe from a screenshot.
  5. Do it early. Once the app shuts down, its data is deleted, and there is no guarantee the public recipe pages will stay up either.

What made Mealime good, and what to look for in a replacement

People loved Mealime for a short list of things. Check any replacement against them:

  • Fast weekly planning. Pick a few dinners and the week is sorted.
  • A grocery list that builds itself, grouped so shopping is quick.
  • Simple, quick recipes for weeknights.
  • Diet preferences that filter what you see.

If you want the least change: Meals Hub

It is Mealime's official next home, and the easiest move if you already shop at a store in the Albertsons family. If you don't, it is less useful.

If you want a planner for your own recipes: Plan to Eat or Paprika

Both import recipes from websites and turn a weekly plan into a shopping list. Plan to Eat is a subscription with a strong planning calendar; Paprika is a one-time purchase per platform with a long track record.

If you want it free: Samsung Food

Samsung Food (formerly Whisk) plans meals and builds shopping lists at no cost, with an optional paid tier.

If you want help at the stove too: Glutt

Glutt is built around the recipes you save, from anywhere. It checks them against what is in your fridge and pantry, plans your week, and puts only what you are missing on the grocery list. Then it goes a step further than Mealime did: Polly, Glutt's live voice AI chef, talks you through the recipe hands-free while you cook. Glutt is not a one-for-one Mealime clone. It has no big built-in library of quick weeknight plans generated for you. If that library was the whole point for you, try Meals Hub first.

Frequently asked questions

When is Mealime shutting down?

Mealime says it will be discontinued as of October 21, 2026.

Can I export my recipes from Mealime?

Mealime's closing notice does not mention an export, and it says personal data will be deleted at shutdown. Mealime's recipes are public pages on mealime.com, so you can import them into another app by link before October 21, and screenshot anything else you want to keep.

What happens to my Mealime Pro subscription?

Mealime says Pro subscribers will not be charged any more subscription fees before the shutdown, and that all Pro recipes and features are available to all users until then.

What is the best free alternative to Mealime?

Samsung Food is a free meal planner with shopping lists. Meals Hub, Mealime's official successor, is included in Albertsons Companies grocery apps.

Can Glutt import Mealime recipes?

Mealime's recipes are on public pages at mealime.com, and Glutt imports recipes from websites. Share the recipe page to Glutt from Safari, or paste its link. For anything that only exists inside the Mealime app, a screenshot works too.
