About

About Glutt.

Most people are not short of recipes. They are short of the help between saving one and eating it. Glutt exists for that gap.

What Glutt is

Glutt: Recipes & AI Chef is an iPhone cooking app with Polly, a live voice AI chef that talks you through any recipe hands-free, answers questions out loud, runs your timers and can look at your pan through the camera. It saves recipes from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit and any website, keeps track of what is in your kitchen, and works with Ray-Ban Meta glasses for audio.

Why we built it

Everyone we know saves food content all day: reels, TikToks, screenshots, bookmarked blogs. Then at 6 pm they still ask "what should I cook?" and order in. The recipes were never the problem. The problem is everything around them: finding the one you saved, knowing whether you have the ingredients, and getting through it on a weeknight without ruining it.

So Glutt is built as a kitchen sidekick, not a recipe database. It turns what you save into recipes you can cook, checks them against what is in your kitchen, and then stays with you at the stove. That last part is Polly, the AI chef, who answers the questions you would otherwise stop and wash your hands to type.

What we believe

  • AI should show up as help, not as a chat box. A good answer at the right step beats a clever essay.
  • Your recipe comes first. Polly works from the recipe you chose, so her help can be checked against it.
  • Nutrition is optional. Calories and protein are there if you want them. No food diary, no daily goals, no guilt.
  • Real kitchens are messy. Wet hands, missing ingredients, a pan about to burn. The app is designed for that moment.
