Questions

Are the ingredients really included?

Yes. The $109.99 covers the session and the ingredients for the recipe you choose. There's no separate grocery bill.

Can I pick any recipe?

Yes — pick something you've always wanted to cook. If it needs anything unusual, we'll let you know before your session.

Do I need to be good at cooking?

No. You cook, and help is there the whole time. The Meta glasses can also walk you through the recipe hands-free.

Who actually comes to my home?

Malik, the founder of Glutt. He runs these Miami sessions himself and stays for the full two hours.

Can someone cook with me?

The session is yours rather than a class, and the price is for the session. If you'd like someone to cook with you, email hi@cielpm.ai before you book so we can plan the shopping around it.

What happens after I book?

You'll get a confirmation email and a calendar invite. We'll reach out before your session if we need anything else from you.

When do I pay?

Checking times costs nothing. You pay when you confirm the booking — card payment is handled by Stripe through Calendly.

What if I need to change the time?

Your confirmation email includes links to reschedule or cancel. If anything comes up, email hi@cielpm.ai and we'll work it out.

Where do you come?

We come to you in Miami. If you're outside the city and not sure, email hi@cielpm.ai and we'll confirm before you book.

Do I need special equipment?

A working kitchen is enough. Tell us what you'd like to cook and we'll let you know if it needs anything you might not have.

Any allergies or foods you avoid?

Mention it when you book, or email hi@cielpm.ai, and we'll plan the shopping around it.
